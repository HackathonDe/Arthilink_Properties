    Property guides

    Buying land safely in Kenya

    Visit the land with the seller, confirm beacons against the survey map, and ask for the title deed and ID of the registered owner.

    Run an official land registry search and have an advocate review the sale agreement before you pay anything. Pay through traceable channels, never cash to individuals.

    What “verified” means here

    A seller identity check or an uploaded title deed is not the same as an independently confirmed legal ownership search. Always confirm ownership through the official land registry processes and use a qualified advocate.

    Rental yield in two steps

    Yearly rent divided by the purchase price, times 100, gives the gross yield. Subtract management, vacancy and repairs for a more realistic net figure.

    Choosing a short-stay location

    Check distance to the beach or city centre, access road quality, security, and whether power and water are reliable before booking.
