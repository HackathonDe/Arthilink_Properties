1. Who we are

Arthilink Properties is the data controller for the personal information collected through this website. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it and what choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

2. What we collect

Account details: your email address, phone number, name and password. Your password is stored in protected (hashed) form and we cannot read it.
What you tell us: messages you send, how you heard about us, and the details and photos of land you submit to sell, including your contact preferences.
Activity on the website: which listings you open, and when you tap Call or WhatsApp, so we can follow up and understand which land people want.
Records of your agreement: the date you accepted these terms and the version you accepted.
Sales records: if you reserve or buy land, we also keep buyer details, payments and receipts.
On your device: a small amount of local storage to remember your light or dark mode choice and to keep you signed in.

3. Why we use it

To create and secure your account; to show you full listing details; to answer your messages and book site visits; to review and publish land you send to sell; to prepare reservations, sale agreements, invoices and receipts; to follow up on enquiries; to improve the website; to prevent fraud and abuse; and to meet our legal duties. Our basis for this is your consent, the need to carry out a contract with you or take steps you request, our legitimate interest in running a safe business, and legal obligations.

4. Marketing

We will only send you marketing messages if you ask for them. You can stop at any time by telling us, and you can ignore the “How did you hear about us?” question without any effect on your account.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with: our own staff who need it for their work; our technology provider that hosts the website and database (Supabase); WhatsApp or your phone network when you choose to contact us through them; lawyers, surveyors or land registry officers when needed to complete a sale you have asked for; and authorities where the law requires. Our hosting provider may store data on servers outside Kenya. Where that happens we take steps to make sure your data stays protected.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account details while your account is open. Enquiries and messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with them and for a reasonable period afterwards. Sales, payment and tax records are kept for the period the law requires. When data is no longer needed we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Keeping it safe

Data is stored in a protected database with access rules, so customers can only see their own information and only authorised staff can see customer records. Connections to the website are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it. If a data breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you may ask to be told what data we hold about you, see it, correct it, have it deleted, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw your consent, and receive a copy of your data. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will reply within the time allowed by law. Some records, for example completed sales, may need to be kept even if you ask for deletion.

9. Cookies and similar tools

The website uses local browser storage to remember your display choice and your signed-in session. We do not use advertising cookies on this website. If this changes we will update this policy and ask for your consent where needed.

10. Children

The website is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us data, please contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a significant change, we will tell you on the website.

12. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first and we will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya (www.odpc.go.ke).
